# Points & savings system

LessonHub uses a dual-currency system to track your progress: **Points** for the leaderboard and **Savings (Euros)** for your personal "bank."

## How points work

Points determine your rank on the leaderboard. They reward **quality** and **speed**.

### Earning points
- **Graded Assignments:** You earn points for every assignment your teacher grades. Higher scores mean more points.
- **Extra Points:** Teachers can award bonus points for going above and beyond.
- **Gold Stars:** A special award that adds a large number of points, based on the star's value.

### Losing points (penalties)
- **Extensions:** Asking for a deadline extension costs **200 points**.
- **Composer Retries:** In Composer lessons, using extra tries to solve a puzzle costs **50 points per try**.
- **News Article penalties:** Penalties are based on unique words tapped (words longer than 4 letters).
- **News Article (>80% tapped):** **2×** the lesson price.
- **News Article (50 to 80% tapped):** **0.3%** of the lesson price.
- **News Article (<50% tapped):** **0.5%** of the lesson price.

---

## How savings (Euros) work

Savings track the **monetary value** of the lessons you complete. Points track your score.

### Growing your savings
- **Completing Lessons:** When a lesson is graded, its **price** is added to your savings.
- **Gold Stars:** Every Gold Star has a Euro value (e.g., €5, €10) that is added to your savings right away.
- **Extra Points:** Bonus points awarded by teachers are converted into small Euro amounts.

### Losing savings
- **Failed Lessons:** If you fail an assignment, the lesson's price is **deducted** from your savings.
- **Composer Retries:** Each extra try costs **€50** in simulated value.
- **News Article penalties:** Penalties are based on unique words tapped (words longer than 4 letters).
- **News Article (>80% tapped):** **2×** the lesson price.
- **News Article (50 to 80% tapped):** **0.3%** of the lesson price.
- **News Article (<50% tapped):** **0.5%** of the lesson price.

---

## The Gold Star

The Gold Star is the highest honor for a single assignment. It adds to both of your totals:
1.  **Euro Value:** It adds a specific amount (e.g., €10) to your Savings.
2.  **Points:** It adds points to your Leaderboard score.
