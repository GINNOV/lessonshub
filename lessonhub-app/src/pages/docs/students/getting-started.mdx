# Getting started

LessonHub is where students, teachers, and admins work on lessons. This page covers sign-in, roles, navigation, and your first profile setup.

## Access requirements

- **Browser:** Latest Safari, Chrome, or Edge. Mobile Safari is supported.
- **Account:** New students are invited by their teacher. Teachers and admins receive credentials from LessonHub staff.
- **Network:** A stable broadband or LTE connection is enough.

## Sign in

1. Visit `https://quantifythis.com`.
2. Select **Sign in**.
3. Enter the email and password from your welcome email.
4. If prompted, confirm via email verification.

## Roles at a glance

| Role | Landing View | Key Abilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Student** | [My Lessons](/my-lessons) | Complete assignments, explore Hub Guides, track progress. |
| **Teacher** | [Teacher dashboard](/dashboard) | Assign lessons, grade work, manage students. |
| **Admin** | [Admin dashboard](/dashboard) | Manage users, system settings, emails, and cron jobs. |

## Navigation basics

- **Top Navigation:** Click your avatar to access your profile, sign out, or view account status.
- **Tabs:** Use the pill-style tabs (e.g., "Lessons / Hub Guides") to switch views. The active tab is highlighted.
- **Cards:** Content is organized into cards. Hover over a card on desktop or tap it on mobile.

## Essential first step

After your first sign-in, go to **Profile → Update Profile** and do the following:
*   Set your **Timezone**. Deadlines and reminders use it.
*   Upload a **Photo**.
*   Confirm your **Preferred Name**.
