# Student dashboard

Your [My Lessons](/my-lessons) dashboard is the first page you see after you sign in. It shows your lessons, progress, and deadlines on any screen size.

![Student Dashboard Overview](/docs/student-dashboard-overview.png)

## Stats header

![My Progress Stats](/docs/my-progress.png)

At the top, you see a quick summary of your progress:
- **Value Earned / Total Points:** The value and points you have earned since you joined.
- **Assignment Chips:** Filters for **Pending**, **Submitted**, **Graded**, **Failed**, and **Past Due**. The counts update in real time.
- **Settings Link:** Check here for teacher notices or pacing guidelines.

## Tabs: lessons & Hub Guides

![Dashboard Tabs](/docs/dashboard-tabs.png)

- **Lessons Tab:** Your main view. Use the **search bar** to find lessons by title or teacher.
- **Hub Guides Tab:** Available if you have an active plan. Browse extra learning materials here.

## Assignment cards

![Dashboard Filters](/docs/dashboard-filters.png)

Each lesson appears as a card:
- **Cover Image:** The lesson's cover picture.
- **Status Badge:** Shows whether the work is Pending, Graded, or Overdue.
- **Difficulty Meter:** Filled bars show how challenging the lesson is.
- **Footer Actions:**
    - **Teacher Avatar:** Click to visit the teacher's profile.
    - **Share:** Copy the lesson link or share via your device.
    - **Practice:** Launch flashcards or quizzes.
    - **Deadline:** Shown in 24-hour format. Turns **red** when overdue.

## Gamification: points & rewards

Here is how points are added and deducted:

### Earning points
- **Graded Assignments:** You earn points based on your score.
- **Gold Stars:** Teachers can award a **Gold Star** for exceptional work. A Gold Star adds to both your **Points** and your **Euro Savings**.

### Penalties (watch out!)
Some actions cost points, the way they would cost money in real life:
- **Extensions:** Need more time? Asking for a deadline extension costs **200 points**.
- **Composer Lessons:** In these special lessons, you have a set number of tries. Every extra try costs **50 points**.

### Practical examples
> **Scenario A: The Perfect Run**
> You complete an assignment perfectly and get **+100 points**. Your savings grow!
>
> **Scenario B: The Extension**
> You do great work (+100 points) but you asked for an extension (-200 points).
> Result: **-100 points**. It is better to submit on time!
>
> **Scenario C: Composer Tries**
> You are stuck on a hard question and use 3 extra tries.
> 3 extra tries x 50 points = **-150 points**.
> Even if you get the answer right, those attempts cost you.

## Games

![Games Invasion](/docs/games-invasion.png)

- Accessible via the **Games** tab or the "Play Games" card.
- Games like "Vocabolario Invaders" are arcade-style games for practicing vocabulary.
- You can choose the difficulty and the visual and audio modes.

## Leaderboard

![Leaderboard](/docs/leaderboard.png)

See where you stand among your peers:
- **Desktop:** View a full table with Rank, Speed Tier, and Savings.
- **Mobile:** A compact card view.
- **Savings Trends:**
    - **Green:** Positive savings (Good job!).
    - **Red:** Negative savings (Time to earn some points!).
    - **Gray:** Break-even.

## Hub Guide banner

- **Premium Students:** You can dismiss the banner after reading it.
- **Free Members:** The banner links to **Profile → Billing**, where you can unlock the full library.
