# Assignments & lessons

This page covers how lessons are created, assigned, and completed.

## Lesson types

- **Standard (Topic):** open-response questions with optional attachments and notes.
- **Flashcard:** flip-card practice; teachers can add visuals and students work through the deck.
- **Multi-choice:** multiple-choice questions with defined correct answers and optional notes.
- **Lyric:** audio-focused lessons with timed lyrics (LRC optional) and downloads.
- **Composer:** answer prompts to reveal a hidden sentence by selecting the correct words.
- **Guides (Hub Guides / Learning Sessions):** multi-step learning cards shown in the Hub Guides tab; can include audio, attachments, and lyric support.

## Student workflow

1. **Open a card** from `/my-lessons` to reach `/assignments/[assignmentId]`.
2. **Read the context:** Lesson Content View shows previews, instructions, attachments, and audio in the same layout for every lesson.
3. **Complete the work:** flashcards, multi-choice, lyric lessons, or standard responses.
4. **Submit:** the footer confirms submission and shows grading status once your teacher reviews it.

## Sharing lessons

- Tap the share icon on any student card.
- On iOS Safari, the native share sheet appears. On other browsers, LessonHub copies the link to your clipboard and shows a toast.
- Each lesson has a persistent share ID; teachers can regenerate it from the assignment form if needed.

## Teacher creation tools

All lesson editors are under the teacher dashboard and share the same controls:

### Lesson form

- Title, preview, price, and difficulty.
- Assignment image upload through the icon-based file picker or Image Browser.
- Optional audio and notes fields.
- Instruction booklets replace or append reusable instructions.

### Multi-Choice creator

- Add questions inline or import from CSV.
- Upload assignment artwork with the same Upload button.
- Attach reading material or SoundCloud/Youtube audio.
- Manage answer options, correctness, and per-question notes.

### Flashcard creator

- Import from CSV or add cards manually.
- Upload artwork for the lesson and individual card sides.
- Preview automatically sorts cards when students practice.

### Guide creator (Hub Guides)

- Create multi-step learning cards with fields content1 to content4 plus extra notes.
- Import entire sessions from CSV using the Upload button.
- Pick a guide cover from the built-in images or upload your own.

## Grading & feedback

- Teachers grade from `/dashboard/grade/[assignmentId]`.
- Students see scores, per-question comments, and overall notes in `/assignments/[assignmentId]`.
- Points go to the Gamification and Leaderboard modules.
